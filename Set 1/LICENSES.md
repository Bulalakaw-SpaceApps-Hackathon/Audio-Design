## Evidence and changes

* Twelve Kenney files were SHA-256 matched to bundled ZIP contents; each pack's License.txt declares CC0.
* Wind and Radio Noise 1 license declarations are supported by Jargon's supplied page excerpts in Set 1-License CHeck.txt.
* Door and recall source pages were independently retrieved and display CC0.
* Space Echo's indexed OpenGameArt page was retrieved on November 15, 2026 and identifies Centurion\_of\_war, CC0 and space\_echo.ogg. Direct page access remains blocked. This supersedes the earlier missing-page status and incorrect Kenney attribution.
* Wind, door, recall and static were converted to Ogg; file mappings follow the preserved selection record. Converted recordings were not independently matched to their originals.
